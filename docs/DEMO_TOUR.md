# Demo tour

A narrated, captioned tour of the suite, with one short video for each major section. It is a **dated snapshot**: where the app and the tour disagree, the app is right.

The videos and transcripts are in the [wiki](https://github.com/quitters/synthograsizer/wiki/Demo-Tour); this page lists what is there. Caption files for each section (WebVTT and SRT, timed from the start of that section's video) are in [`demo-tour/captions/`](demo-tour/captions/).

## The sections

| Section | What it covers | Length |
|---|---|---|
| [Perform](https://github.com/quitters/synthograsizer/wiki/Demo-Tour-Perform) | The prompt playground: knobs, the D-pad and the template library, plus batch prompt export. | 0:52 |
| [Template Gen](https://github.com/quitters/synthograsizer/wiki/Demo-Tour-Template-Gen) | Turn an idea, a story or a reference image into a template, remix an existing one, or generate a live p5.js sketch. | 2:52 |
| [Generate and Automate](https://github.com/quitters/synthograsizer/wiki/Demo-Tour-Generate-and-Automate) | Style transfer, image and video batches, and a chained workflow. | 2:20 |
| [Agents](https://github.com/quitters/synthograsizer/wiki/Demo-Tour-Agents) | Build an agent from a description, then put a team of them in a session and steer the conversation. | 0:59 |
| [Glitcher](https://github.com/quitters/synthograsizer/wiki/Demo-Tour-Glitcher) | The glitch-art studio: effects, an effect confined to a drawn selection, before and after, and focus mode. | 0:39 |
| [The Commons](https://github.com/quitters/synthograsizer/wiki/Demo-Tour-The-Commons) | A shared canvas: the host's desk, the wall, and a guest's phone, then a piece generated from a prompt. | 0:33 |

About 8:31 in all. Each section has its own page with the video, a table of its chapters, the captions as separate files, and a transcript.

- **Recorded:** 2026-09-29 against `8e7204a`; the Agents session chapter, the Glitcher and the Commons were recorded 2026-10-05 and 2026-10-06 against `3bb2e34`.
- **Narration:** Gemini text-to-speech (voice *Kore*). Captions are burned into every video, and each page has the full transcript.
- **What it shows:** a local install, where everything is unlocked and nothing needs a sign-in. The hosted service at synthograsizer.com spends credits on AI calls and keeps Video and Music for the operator; see the [README](https://github.com/quitters/synthograsizer#readme).
- **Not shown:** the Commons archive library (its licence decisions are still open, see [COMPLIANCE_ROADMAP.md](https://github.com/quitters/synthograsizer/blob/main/docs/COMPLIANCE_ROADMAP.md)) and anything that needs the hosted service's account screens.

## Notes on the footage

- Generated results (images, templates, sketches, the Commons piece, the agents' conversation) differ every time.
