Make your first experiment.

  1. Start with media. Import an image, GIF, or short video—or try the sample.
  2. Add an effect. Explore a category or search by name. Effects appear in your stack.
  3. Tune and combine. Select a stack card for its controls. Toggle effects off or solo one to explore it.
  4. Choose where it lands. Use automatic areas or paint a selection for the selected effect.
  5. Keep a frame. Pause to catch a moment, compare with the original, then export.

Some effects build up over time. Reset image restores source pixels while keeping your effects. The classic editor includes additional legacy filters and export options.
